Website setup

Oudkempeneet Eetcafé · oudkempeneetcafe.nl

Before you start

  • The website folder we sent you, saved to your Desktop
  • Your phone
  • Your login for Key-Systems, where your domain is registered

1. Create a Cloudflare account

  1. Go to dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up
  2. Enter your email and a password, tick Verify you are human, click Sign up
  3. Open the email from Cloudflare and click the confirmation link
  4. Set up two-factor authentication when prompted, using your phone
  5. Print the recovery codes it shows you and keep them in the café
Without those recovery codes, a lost phone means the account is locked permanently. Nobody can reopen it — not Cloudflare, not us.

2. Upload the website

  1. Left menu → Compute → Workers & Pages
  2. Create application → Pages tab → Upload assets
  3. Project name:oudkempeneet-cafe
  4. Drag in the folder we sent you, then click Deploy site
  5. Click the .pages.dev link and check the site loads

3. Add your domain

  1. Click the Cloudflare logo (top left) → Add a domain
  2. Enter:oudkempeneetcafe.nl
  3. Choose the Free plan
  4. On the records screen, check there is a TXT record reading v=spf1 -all, then click Continue
  5. Write down the two nameservers it gives you — they look like anna.ns.cloudflare.com

4. Change the nameservers

  1. Log in to Key-Systems, or the reseller you bought the domain from
  2. Find the Nameservers setting
  3. Remove these four: ns-cloud-e1.googledomains.com ns-cloud-e2.googledomains.com ns-cloud-e3.googledomains.com ns-cloud-e4.googledomains.com
  4. Add the two Cloudflare nameservers from step 3.5
  5. Save
Keep this page. Those four values are how you undo everything — see the last section.

5. Connect the domain to the site

  1. Compute → Workers & Pages → oudkempeneet-cafe
  2. Custom domains tab → Set up a custom domain
  3. Add oudkempeneetcafe.nl
  4. Repeat for www.oudkempeneetcafe.nl

6. Check it works

Wait up to an hour, then confirm all four:

  • https://oudkempeneetcafe.nl loads, with a padlock
  • https://www.oudkempeneetcafe.nl loads, with a padlock
  • The site looks right on your phone
  • A test reservation arrives in your email
Tell us when all four pass. Do not continue to step 7 before that.

7. Cancel Squarespace

  1. Squarespace → Settings → Import/Export → Export, and save the file
  2. Save any photos from the old site you want to keep
  3. Cancel the subscription

8. Updating the site later

  1. We email you a new folder
  2. Compute → Workers & Pages → oudkempeneet-cafe
  3. Create a new deployment, drag the folder in

To undo everything

Set your nameservers at Key-Systems back to:

ns-cloud-e1.googledomains.com ns-cloud-e2.googledomains.com ns-cloud-e3.googledomains.com ns-cloud-e4.googledomains.com

Your site returns to how it was, usually within the hour. This works while the Squarespace subscription is still active.
